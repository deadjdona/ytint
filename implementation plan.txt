# YouTube Comment Corpus — Comprehensive Implementation Plan

This implementation plan maps the 14 analytical families of `description_long.md` into the production architecture of **ytint**: a deterministic 53-stage analytical pipeline (`s00_ingest.py` through `s51_topic_injection.py`, plus `s99_visualize.py`), a modular visualization package (`src/pipeline/visualizations/`), and an interactive 7-tab Streamlit intelligence dashboard (`src/ui/app.py`).

---

## 🏗️ Production Architecture Overview

The pipeline is organized into **7 sequential phases** enforced by the orchestrator (`src/pipeline/runner.py`), utilizing Snappy-compressed Apache Parquet tables and Rust-accelerated BPE tokenization:

- **Phase 1: Ingestion & Core NLP / Semantics (`s00`–`s08`)**: Data standardization, multilingual sentiment, topic modeling (BERTopic), entity extraction, co-occurrence networks, intent classification, stance drift, and code-switching.
- **Phase 2: Conversational Tree & Thread Dynamics (`s09`–`s15`)**: Thread width/branching, reply latency, position bias, resolution patterns, initiator/responder roles, polarization, and toxicity contagion ($R_0$).
- **Phase 3: Author Profiling & Forensics (`s16`–`s27`)**: Interaction networks, bipartite graphs, co-commenting, RFM author aggregation, frequency tiers, drive-by vs loyalists, stylometrics, creator impersonation, bot heuristics, CIB rings, integrity flags, and like inflation.
- **Phase 4: Video Dynamics, Cohorts & Longitudinal Topology (`s28`–`s37`)**: Change-point narrative timelines, cross-modal MM:SS mapping, like shelf life decay, topic evolution, topic-video matrices, cohort retention, audience overlap, topic cohorts, arrival velocity, and comparative radar profiles.
- **Phase 5: Predictive Modeling & Causal Inference (`s38`–`s40`)**: Gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost), Tree SHAP attributions, Kaplan-Meier thread survival, Poisson bursts, Difference-in-Differences (DiD) creator uplift, and UI metric synthesis.
- **Phase 5b: Extended Supplementary Analysis (`s41`–`s51`)**: TF-IDF keywords, polarity vs engagement, emoji signatures, sentiment anomaly spikes, corpus quality, within-thread topic drift, slang lexicons, Bow-Tie graph decomposition, series vs standalone, scene reactions/spoilers, and topic injection detection.
- **Phase 6: Final Visualizations & Reporting (`s99`)**: Publication-ready gallery of 68+ statistical plots across 5 dedicated modules (`nlp_plots.py`, `thread_plots.py`, `author_plots.py`, `temporal_plots.py`, `model_plots.py`).

## 🗺️ Stage-by-Stage Implementation Matrix

### Phase 1: Ingestion, NLP & Demand Intent (`s00`–`s08`)
- **`s00_ingest.py`**: Ingests SQLite (`commentsuite.sqlite3`), extracts text, metadata, clean timestamps. Writes `comments_clean.parquet` and `videos_clean.parquet`.
- **`s01_enrich.py`**: Computes VADER sentiment, RubERT/XLM-R continuous valence, GoEmotions affect labels, Detoxify toxicity, textstat readability (Flesch), lexical diversity (TTR, MTLD, Yule's K), and timestamp mentions. Writes `.s01_complete`.
- **`s02_topics.py`**: Embeds comments (`paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2`), clusters via UMAP + HDBSCAN, extracts c-TF-IDF topics, and computes Procrustes-aligned Word2Vec semantic drift. Writes `topic_metadata.parquet` and `semantic_drift.parquet`.
- **`s03_ner.py`**: Extracts proper named entities (`PER`, `ORG`, `LOC`, `PRODUCT`, `EVENT`) via spaCy transformers. Writes `named_entities.parquet`.
- **`s04_cooccurrence.py`**: Builds word co-occurrence networks with PMI weighting. Writes `word_cooccurrence_edges.parquet` and `word_cooccurrence_nodes.parquet`.
- **`s05_tags_mentions.py`**: Extracts hashtags and user-to-user `@` mentions into interaction graphs. Writes `tag_network_edges.parquet` and `tag_network_nodes.parquet`.
- **`s06_audience_intent.py`**: Classifies audience comments into 6 strategic intents (Feature Requests, Technical Questions, Praise, Bug Reports, Debates). Writes `audience_intent_summary.parquet` and `audience_content_requests.parquet`.
- **`s07_stance_drift.py`**: Infers entity-level stance (Favor, Against, Neutral) and evaluates ideological polarization drift across tree debate depth. Writes `stance_summary.parquet`, `stance_depth_drift.parquet`, and `polarized_threads.parquet`.
- **`s08_code_switching.py`**: Detects bilingual text mixing (Cyrillic/Latin) and evaluates engagement lift. Writes `code_switching_impact.parquet`.

### Phase 2: Conversational Tree & Thread Dynamics (`s09`–`s15`)
- **`s09_thread_width.py`**: Computes thread width, branching factor distributions, and attention transfer ratios. Writes `thread_width_dist.parquet` and `attention_transfer.parquet`.
- **`s10_reply_latency.py`**: Computes response time intervals between comments and parent roots ($\Delta t$). Writes `reply_latency.parquet`.
- **`s11_position_bias.py`**: Quantifies the upvote advantage of early comments vs comment rank order. Writes `position_bias.parquet`.
- **`s12_resolution_patterns.py`**: Classifies terminal thread leaves into consensus, agreement, hostility, or abandonment. Writes `resolution_patterns.parquet`.
- **`s13_initiator_patterns.py`**: Profiles linguistic and emotional differences between thread initiators and responders. Writes `initiator_patterns.parquet`.
- **`s14_polarization.py`**: Analyzes sentiment divergence and thread sentiment decay slopes across contentious debate trees. Writes `thread_polarization_corpus.parquet`.
- **`s15_toxicity_contagion.py`**: Models epidemiological toxicity reproduction numbers ($R_0$) and identifies troll catalyst accounts. Writes `toxicity_contagion_summary.parquet`, `troll_catalysts.parquet`, and `video_toxicity_contagion.parquet`.

### Phase 3: Author Loyalty, Network Topology & Forensics (`s16`–`s27`)
- **`s16_network.py`**: Builds directed reply graphs, computing PageRank, in/out degrees, and Louvain modularity clusters. Writes `authors_network_metrics.parquet`.
- **`s17_bipartite.py`**: Projects two-mode bipartite networks connecting authors to videos. Writes `bipartite_edges.parquet` and `bipartite_nodes.parquet`.
- **`s18_cocommenting.py`**: Identifies audience sub-communities via Jaccard similarity of comment co-occurrence. Writes `cocomment_edges.parquet` and `cocomment_nodes.parquet`.
- **`s19_aggregation.py`**: Core aggregation layer producing `authors_final.parquet` (RFM scores, loyalty metrics) and `videos_final.parquet` (Gini attention inequality, attention half-life).
- **`s20_frequency_tiers.py`**: Segments authors into commenting frequency tiers (One-off, Casual, Regular, Super-Fan). Writes `frequency_tiers.parquet`.
- **`s21_driveby_loyalists.py`**: Segments one-off casual commenters from cross-video community loyalists. Writes `driveby_loyalists.parquet`.
- **`s22_author_fingerprints.py`**: Generates multi-axis radar stylometric fingerprints for top community contributors. Writes `author_fingerprints.parquet`.
- **`s23_impersonation_detection.py`**: Flags unauthorized accounts copying creator channel names. Writes `impersonation_detection.parquet`.
- **`s24_bot_heuristics.py`**: Identifies automated spam bots using volume vs uniqueness ratios, name reuse, and Gigatoken BPE sequence hashing. Writes `bot_classifications.parquet`.
- **`s25_cib_detection.py`**: Uncovers astroturfing rings synchronizing comments within $\Delta t \le 120\text{s}$ across multiple uploads. Writes `cib_rings.parquet` and `cib_coordinated_comments.parquet`.
- **`s26_integrity.py`**: Scans for MinHash LSH near-duplicate spam clusters and temporal burst brigading. Writes `integrity_flags.parquet`.
- **`s27_anomaly_inflation.py`**: Detects astroturfed comments with anomalous like-to-reply inflation ratios. Writes `like_inflation.parquet`.

### Phase 4: Video Dynamics, Cohorts & Longitudinal Topology (`s28`–`s37`)
- **`s28_narrative.py`**: Computes narrative time-series, PELT change-points, and viral anomaly spikes ($>2.5\sigma$). Writes `historical_timeline.parquet` and `viral_events.parquet`.
- **`s29_cross_modal.py`**: Maps timestamp mentions to exact video playback runtimes (MM:SS). Writes `video_reaction_map.parquet`.
- **`s30_shelf_life.py`**: Models exponential decay of engagement and like accrual ($t_{1/2}$). Writes `shelf_life_likes.parquet`.
- **`s31_topic_evolution.py`**: Tracks topic interest changes across calendar months. Writes `topic_evolution.parquet`.
- **`s32_topic_matrix.py`**: Constructs clustered heatmap of topics across videos. Writes `topic_video_matrix.parquet`.
- **`s33_cohorts.py`**: Measures long-term retention across monthly acquisition cohorts. Writes `cohort_retention.parquet`, `cohort_retention_pct.parquet`, and `new_vs_returning.parquet`.
- **`s34_overlap.py`**: Computes pairwise Jaccard similarity across video audience commenter sets. Writes `video_overlap_matrix.parquet`.
- **`s35_topic_cohorts.py`**: Identifies which topics attract and retain specific seasonal commenter cohorts. Writes `topic_cohorts.parquet`.
- **`s36_arrival_speed.py`**: Segments commenters into speed tiers (First Responders, Early Day, Long-Tail) and builds minute-level arrival velocity curves. Writes `arrival_speed.parquet` and `minute_arrival_curve.parquet`.
- **`s37_cross_video_comparisons.py`**: Generates comparative video radar profiles, 14-day controversy impact windows, and counterfactual synthetic control causal modeling. Writes `video_profile_radar.parquet`, `controversy_impact.parquet`, and `causal_impact_summary.parquet`.

### Phase 5: Predictive, Causal Modeling & Synthesis (`s38`–`s40`)
- **`s38_modeling.py`**: Trains XGBoost like-count regressors with chronological 80/20 train/test splits, Tree SHAP feature attributions, right-censored Kaplan-Meier thread survival, Poisson burst detection, forward engagement forecasting, and Kruskal-Wallis / Dunn's post-hoc pairwise significance testing. Writes 14 predictive models and datasets.
- **`s39_creator_uplift.py`**: Evaluates causal impact of early creator interventions using Difference-in-Differences (DiD) quasi-experiments. Writes `creator_causal_uplift.parquet` and `creator_intervention_threads.parquet`.
- **`s40_synthesis.py`**: Pre-computes and indexes topic metadata in-place for high-speed dashboard loading. Updates `topic_metadata.parquet`.

### Phase 5b: Extended Supplementary Analysis (`s41`–`s51`)
- **`s41_tfidf_keywords.py`**: Computes video-specific TF-IDF keyword extraction and salient differentiating terms. Writes `tfidf_keywords.parquet`.
- **`s42_polarity_engagement.py`**: Evaluates whether controversial or negative comments achieve higher engagement yields. Writes `polarity_engagement.parquet`.
- **`s43_emoji_signatures.py`**: Extracts emoji usage distributions and builds an empirical emoji-to-sentiment mapping. Writes `emoji_signatures.parquet`.
- **`s44_sentiment_anomalies.py`**: Detects sudden hourly and daily negativity spikes crossing standard deviation thresholds ($>2.5\sigma$). Writes `sentiment_anomalies.parquet`.
- **`s45_corpus_quality.py`**: Validates corpus completeness, comment disable flags, HTTP response codes, and language coverage. Writes `corpus_quality.parquet` and `language_coverage.parquet`.
- **`s46_thread_topic_drift.py`**: Tracks semantic and thematic drift across conversational reply chains relative to the root topic. Writes `thread_topic_drift.parquet` and `video_topic_drift_summary.parquet`.
- **`s47_slang_lexicon.py`**: Analyzes internet slang, gaming jargon, and neologisms. Writes `slang_lexicon_frequency.parquet`.
- **`s48_bowtie_concentration.py`**: Decomposes the author-reply network into Bow-Tie components (SCC, IN, OUT, Tendrils/Tubes) and Top-K concentration ratios. Writes `network_bowtie_structure.parquet` and `top_k_concentration.parquet`.
- **`s49_series_creator_sentiment.py`**: Benchmarks audience engagement on episodic series vs standalone uploads and computes creator net sentiment polarity. Writes `series_vs_standalone.parquet` and `creator_sentiment_polarity.parquet`.
- **`s50_cross_modal_reactions.py`**: Classifies reactions into a 5-class scene taxonomy (Humor, Shock, Emotional, Critique, Navigation) and detects spoilers. Writes `cross_modal_scene_reactions.parquet` and `spoiler_detections.parquet`.
- **`s51_topic_injection.py`**: Identifies astroturfed topic injections and thematic hijacking using Jensen-Shannon and Kullback-Leibler divergence monitors. Writes `topic_injection_anomalies.parquet`.

### Phase 6: Final Visualizations Gallery (`s99`)
- **`s99_visualize.py`**: Modular visualization package in `src/pipeline/visualizations/` containing **68 distinct publication-quality plot generators**:
  - `nlp_plots.py` (15 plots): UMAP embeddings, co-occurrence networks, NER distributions, sarcasm, linguistic profiling, tag networks, code-switching, audience intent, stance drift, emoji treemaps, length vs likes hexbins, language distributions, valence per topic, slang lexicons, TF-IDF keywords.
  - `thread_plots.py` (11 plots): Reply depth, polarization dynamics, latency distributions, position bias, arrival speed, thread width, resolution patterns, initiator patterns, toxicity contagion, thread sunburst, corpus quality.
  - `author_plots.py` (15 plots): Pareto activity, force-directed networks, 3D RFM, Lorenz curves, bipartite graphs, co-commenting, frequency tiers, bot heuristics, drive-by loyalists, like inflation, stylometric fingerprints, impersonation, CIB rings, Bow-Tie structure, Top-K concentration.
  - `temporal_plots.py` (20 plots): Diurnal heatmaps, Plutchik radar, sentiment ridges, sentiment divergence, toxicity heatmaps, STL decomposition, reaction timelines, anomaly scatter, topic streamgraphs, shelf life decay, half-life curves, velocity spikes, topic-video matrices, cohort retention, audience overlap, new vs returning share, topic cohorts, cross-video benchmarking, topic injections, minute-level arrival curves.
  - `model_plots.py` (7 plots): Kaplan-Meier survival curves, Tree SHAP attributions, creator causal uplift, Poisson bursts, series vs standalone benchmarks, creator sentiment polarity, cross-modal scene reactions.
